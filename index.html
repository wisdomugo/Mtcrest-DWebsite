Our story

A school built on purpose, not compromise

Mountain Crest Schools was founded with a single conviction: that every child deserves an education that challenges the mind and nurtures the character. Located in the heart of Owerri, Imo State, we have grown into one of the most trusted model schools in the South-East — built on academic rigour, genuine care, and an unwavering belief in the potential of every student.

We offer both the Nigerian national curriculum (WAEC/NECO) and the Cambridge International pathway simultaneously — giving every family the flexibility to choose the future their child deserves. Our campus is a place of small classes, qualified educators, and a community that holds each child to the highest standard while never letting them feel alone in reaching it.
