About us

Thirteen years of Mountain Crest

Mountain Crest opened in New Owerri in 2013. Thirteen years, two campuses, and twelve year-groups seen through to graduation.

It started as one school with a straightforward idea: teach children well, and know each of them while you do it. That has not changed, though a good deal else has.

Our values

What we stand for

  • Every child known

    Not a year group, not a class list. A child, with a name, whom somebody here is responsible for.

  • Taught to excel

    High expectations, held kindly. Children rise to what is expected of them, and we expect a great deal.

  • Growing in excellence

    Our motto, and it has a verb in it on purpose. Excellence here is something a child moves toward, not a standard they either meet or fail.

  • Our Christian foundation

    Mountain Crest is a Christian school. Worship is part of the ordinary shape of the week, and the values above are held for that reason. Children of other faiths are welcome here and always have been.
