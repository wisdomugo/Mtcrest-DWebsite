The admissions process

Five steps, and we guide you through each one

Every family’s route in is a little different. These are the steps most families take.

There is no deadline to miss — we take enquiries all year and will tell you what is available for your child’s level.

  1. 1. Enquire

    Tell us your child’s name, age and the level you are considering. Use the form below or call us — whichever suits you.

    Start an enquiryCall 0816 097 0026

  2. 2. Visit a campus

    Come and see an ordinary day. You will meet a teacher from your child’s level and see the rooms your child would be in.

    Optional photoA parent and a teacher talking in a corridor or doorway. Faces readable.

    Book a visit

  3. 3. Talk about your child

    We sit down together and talk about your child — what they are like, where they are now, and what they need from us.

  4. 4. Receive an offer

    If Mountain Crest is the right school for your child, we will tell you what happens next and what it costs.

    We will not ask you to commit to anything before you have seen the school.

  5. 5. Complete enrolment

    Paperwork, uniform and a start date. We will walk you through all of it.

What happens on a visit

A visit takes about an hour. Someone shows you round, you meet a teacher from your child’s level, and you can ask anything you like.

FAQs

Questions parents ask us

Something we have not answered? Call 0816 097 0026 or send us a message — we would rather you asked.

What does it cost?

Fees vary by level. Tell us your child’s level and we will send you the current figures. What the fee covers, and what is charged separately, is set out above.

Start an enquiry

What age can my child start?

We teach from Crèche through to High School. Early Years alone covers six stages, so there is almost certainly one that fits your child’s age — tell us how old they are and we will say which.

Which campus would my child attend?

Early Years and Grade School are at No. 3 Eugene Opara Crescent, opposite Index Petrol Station. High School is at No. 7–9 Mountain Crest Avenue, opposite Oputa High Court. Both are in New Owerri.

Do you offer boarding?

Yes, at High School level. Boarding is best discussed rather than read about — tell us you are interested and someone will call you to talk it through.

Is there a waiting list?

It depends on the level and the time of year. Ask us about your child’s level and we will tell you honestly what is available.

Can I visit before deciding?

Please do. Most parents decide after a visit, and we would rather you saw an ordinary day than took our word for it.
